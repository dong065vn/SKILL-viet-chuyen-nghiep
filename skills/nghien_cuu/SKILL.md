## Academic Reading Techniques
### 1. Scanning (Keyword Reading)
- Search for keywords in the reading material
- Read sections containing keyword information carefully
- Determine if the material is relevant to research objectives

### 2. Skimming (Overview Reading)
- Read for overall understanding of the main ideas
- Read first and last paragraphs of each section
- Read abstract and conclusion sections

### 3. Intensive Reading (Detailed Analysis)
- Read deeply and analyze thoroughly
- Note important information:
  - Research objectives
  - Research methodology
  - Sample size
  - Research findings
  - Limitations and future directions

## Knowledge Gap Identification
1. Review related studies systematically
2. Group by theme/methodology/results
3. Identify what has been researched
4. Determine what HAS NOT been researched → **Knowledge Gap**
5. Confirm novelty and necessity of the research
